Two questions. Two seconds.

What are my agents doing right now? Am I about to hit my limits? Nagi answers both from your menu bar or tray.

1 / 6

Every agent, by name.

Each running agent hangs from the agent that started it, with its model, how long it has run and when it last spoke.

2 / 6

One is asking you something.

Its dot turns rose and the line above says 1 asks you. Nothing else on the panel is rose except now.

3 / 6

One has gone quiet.

A line grows under it while it's silent. Past halfway it turns yellow; at 15 minutes it reads silent 15 m and you get one notification.

4 / 6

Limits, one line per account.

The lowest window first, the % left and the reset date. If a check fails it says couldn't check · tried 17:31, never 0%.

5 / 6

When it can't see, it says so.

If T3 Code stops answering, the count greys, the last list stays with its time, and no alarm fires on old news.

6 / 6

Nothing running.

It says when the last one ended. Everything stays on your computer: no cloud, no account, no telemetry.
