Nagi is a small glance in your menu bar or tray for people running AI coding agents. It answers two questions.

Am I about to hit my limits?

One line per account: the lowest window first, the % left and the date it resets. Two Codex accounts stay two lines.

What are my agents doing right now?

Each agent by its title, hanging from the one that started it. A line grows under it while it's quiet.

Demo data, sped up.

It stays on your computer.

No cloud, no account, no telemetry. Nagi reads T3 Code on your own computers with a read-only token and talks to nothing else. It can't stop, send or approve anything.

A small reader answers only you, on 127.0.0.1. To watch another computer, it connects over your own Tailscale.

It says what it knows, and when it doesn't.

Install Nagi

On Linux or macOS, with Python 3.11 or newer. It asks before it uses sudo and never overwrites your data.

git clone https://github.com/yeqiuqiu123/nagi.git && cd nagi && ./install.sh
