Nagi website · five concepts

Creative direction: design/site/creative-direction.md (Fable, call 1). Every board embeds the real panel (site/panel/, synced from web/) on the fictional fixture. Each concept is a working prototype; open it with the link. Screens: opening and the key-journey moment at 1440 and 390, dark.

1. The Line

Idea. The page is the panel’s drawing at page scale: its rose now line leaves the live panel and crosses the page; the sections hang from a trunk.

Problem. A screenshot beside a headline shows the panel but not its grammar.

Refuses. Section cards, icons, a link-bar nav, any filled button.

Identity. Wordmark as the panel header. Panel tokens verbatim. Display in the numeral’s cut. Install is the page’s only “asks you”: a rose node beside the command.

Motion. The story scene plays once; the page line follows the panel’s 700 ms travel exactly; each branch draws once on entering view.

Open the working prototype

2. Two Seconds

Idea. The live panel is pinned while six captions scroll; each caption cuts the panel to its scene.

Problem. Five states make one promise, and a single hero can only show one.

Refuses. Leader lines, crossfades, device frames.

Identity. Captions at title size with the panel’s own strings quoted inline.

Motion. Scene changes are instant cuts on the next frame. Risk: a known scrollytelling template; at 390 it becomes a stack.

Open the working prototype

3. One Object

Idea. One viewport: the panel, one sentence, the command and GitHub. Privacy is a second plain document.

Problem. The README is dense, so the opposite is one screen.

Refuses. Scrolling on the home page, sections, a footer of links.

Identity. The sentence carries everything; the panel is the only image.

Motion. Only the story scene. Risk: explains privacy least; the most forgettable.

Open the working prototype

4. In Place

Idea. An empty desktop in --bg; the tray readout 凪 3 · ● at real size with the panel hanging from it.

Problem. Half the product is the tray readout, and no other concept shows it.

Refuses. Drawn menu-bar chrome, wallpaper, a MacBook.

Identity. The tray label is the logo lockup; its marks are the legend.

Motion. None beyond the panel. Risk: at 390 there is no tray, so the conceit collapses.

Open the working prototype

5. Everything It Says

Idea. An index of the panel’s vocabulary: what Nagi says, when, and what it never says. Choosing a row cuts the panel to that scene.

Problem. Honesty is a claim until it is specified.

Refuses. Adjectives, feature lists, anything not quoted from the code.

Identity. Catalogue typography: numbered rows, tabular alignment, struck-through “never” column.

Motion. Row focus cuts the scene. Risk: rewards the second visit, not the first.
