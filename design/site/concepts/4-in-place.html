It lives up here.

A number in your menu bar, and the panel behind it.

The count is your running agents. A mark after it tells you why to look.

  • 凪 3Three agents running, all fine.
  • 凪 3 · ?One is asking you something.
  • 凪 3 · ●One has been silent past your stuck time.
  • 凪 3 · emptyA limit window is used up.

It stays on your computer.

No cloud, no account, no telemetry. A read-only reader on 127.0.0.1; other computers only over your own Tailscale.
