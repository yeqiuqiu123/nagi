# Nagi site voice

**One developer talking to another across a desk.** Complete, friendly sentences in the second person, with contractions. Plain and exact, the way the panel speaks: dates first, numbers with their units, and states named for what they are. Warmth comes from specifics (a title, a reset date, `silent 15 m`), never from adjectives.

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| You can see which agent went quiet, and for how long. | Real-time AI agent observability. |
| Codex 1 is empty until Tue 17 Nov 05:09. | Smart usage insights. |
| It runs on your computer and sends nothing anywhere. | Enterprise-grade privacy. |
| Coming soon | Roadmap, beta, early access (for things not built) |
| couldn't check · tried 17:31 | Error, 0%, N/A |

- **No hype words:** no "seamless", "powerful", "supercharge", "unlock", "effortless", "blazing", "AI-powered". No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Sentence case everywhere.** Product names keep their casing: T3 Code, Claude Code, oh-my-pi, OpenClaw.
- **The panel's words are the site's words:** running, asks you, silent, stuck, empty, couldn't check, ended.
- **Never claim what main doesn't do.** Unbuilt sources say "Coming soon". No licence is named until the owner picks one.
